Ultimate Guide to Preparing for Punjab, Islamabad, and NHMP Traffic Assistant General Knowledge

Preparing for the Traffic Assistant recruitment exam in Punjab Police, Islamabad Police, or the National Highways & Motorway Police (NHMP) requires a strategic approach to General Knowledge. Since these exams are highly competitive, mastering the GK section is essential to securing your position.

Key Topics to Cover

To excel in the General Knowledge section, candidates should focus on the following core areas:

  • Pakistan Affairs: Key events since 1947, important political figures, constitutional amendments, and historical milestones.
  • Current Affairs: Stay updated on national and international news, specifically focusing on the last six to twelve months.
  • Islamic Studies: Basic knowledge of the Quran, Sunnah, major battles of Islam, and Islamic history.
  • Geography & Science: Basic world geography, major rivers, mountains, and foundational physics, chemistry, and biology concepts taught at the matriculation level.
  • Everyday Science: Focus on inventions, the human body, space, and environmental issues.
  • Traffic Rules and Road Safety: This is a mandatory component for NHMP and Traffic Assistant roles. Study road signs, signaling systems, and the Motorway Ordinance.

Preparation Tips for Success

  • Utilize Past Papers: Solve past papers of Punjab Police and NHMP to understand the paper pattern and frequently repeated questions.
  • Stay Updated: Regularly read national newspapers and visit reliable educational websites for daily updates.
  • Create a Study Schedule: Dedicate at least two hours daily to GK. Consistency is more effective than last-minute cramming.
  • Use Reliable Resources: Refer to standard books like Caravan General Knowledge or Dogar Publishers’ guides, which are tailored for PPSC and FPSC-based police exams.

By focusing on these specific areas and maintaining a consistent revision schedule, you can significantly improve your chances of clearing the written exam. Prioritize road safety signs and current national events, as these carry substantial weight in modern recruitment tests.
