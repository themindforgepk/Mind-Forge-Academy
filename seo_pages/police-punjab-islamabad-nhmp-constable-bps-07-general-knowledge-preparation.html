Complete Guide: General Knowledge Preparation for Police Constable (BPS-07)

Preparing for the Police Constable (BPS-07) exam in Punjab, Islamabad, or the National Highways and Motorway Police (NHMP) requires a strategic approach to General Knowledge. Since the competition is high, your ability to score in the GK section often determines your final selection.

Key Topics to Cover

To excel in the written test, you must focus on these core areas:
  • Pakistan Affairs: Focus on the history of the Pakistan Movement, key personalities, important dates, and the post-1947 political landscape.
  • Islamic Studies: Study the basics of the Quran, Sunnah, Five Pillars of Islam, Battle history, and prominent Sahaba.
  • Geography: Familiarize yourself with Pakistan’s provinces, major rivers, mountain ranges, neighboring countries, and global capitals.
  • Everyday Science: Learn basic concepts of biology, physics, and chemistry, including vitamins, famous inventions, and environmental awareness.
  • Current Affairs: Keep track of the latest national appointments, sports events, and major international news from the last six months.
  • Computer Literacy: Basic questions regarding MS Office, internet usage, and hardware components are common in recent constable exams.

Preparation Tips for Success

Consistency is the key to mastering General Knowledge. Spend at least two hours daily reading reliable sources like "Carvan" or "Dogar" preparation books specifically tailored for NTS/PTS/CTS patterns. Utilize YouTube channels that specialize in past papers, as many questions in constable exams are frequently repeated. Practice solving past papers from 2020-2024 to understand the question difficulty level and time management. When studying, create small flashcards for dates and names to help with quick memorization. Avoid cramming everything at once; instead, categorize your study plan by subject. By focusing on these essential GK topics and practicing regularly, you will significantly improve your chances of passing the Punjab, Islamabad, or NHMP Police Constable (BPS-07) recruitment process. Stay motivated and keep your focus sharp!
